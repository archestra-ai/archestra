Network policy enforcement:
  Two probe pods tested whether this cluster enforces NetworkPolicy, and the
  "[archestra] network policy check" line above carries the result. A warning
  there means egress rules configured in Archestra are accepted and then
  ignored.

  Helm prints that line from 3.18 and 4.0 onwards. On older versions, read it
  from the pods instead:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=netpol-probe

  The pods and their deny-all policy are hook resources, so `helm uninstall`
  leaves them behind. Remove them with:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} delete pod,networkpolicy -l app.kubernetes.io/component=netpol-probe
{{- end }}
