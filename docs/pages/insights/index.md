Insights shows what happens across your organization: every model request, tool call, Guardrails decision, and admin change in one timeline, who has connected agents and how much they use them, and what it all costs. Each page is under **Insights** in the sidebar.

To cap spend or send costs to your FinOps tools, see [Cost Controls](/docs/llm-proxy/cost-controls).
